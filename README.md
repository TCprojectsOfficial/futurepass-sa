Overview

FuturePass SA is an education technology project developed by TCprojects to help South African learners connect their education, interests, skills and achievements with future career opportunities.

The platform is designed to support a learner-development journey that extends beyond academic results. Through a digital Skills Wallet, achievement recognition, digital credentials and career discovery, learners can build a record of their development and explore possible future pathways.

The long-term vision is to connect education, practical skills, qualifications, entrepreneurship and employment opportunities through one learner-focused digital ecosystem.

The problem

Learners need more than academic results to communicate their abilities and explore their future options.

Interests, practical skills, achievements and personal development can all contribute to career readiness, but these elements are not always connected in one accessible digital experience.

FuturePass SA aims to help bridge this gap by providing a structured platform for recognising learner development and exploring career possibilities.

Project objectives

- Give learners a digital space to build a record of their skills and achievements.
- Support school-managed recognition and verification of achievements.
- Provide access to digital credentials and learner portfolios.
- Help learners explore careers based on their interests, subjects and verified skills.
- Create a foundation for practical learning and future qualification pathways.
- Support a longer-term connection between education and economic opportunity.

Key features

Learner accounts and school management

- Learner registration and authenticated access.
- School registration and approval workflows.
- Role-based access for learners, teachers, school administrators and platform administrators.
- School-managed teacher and learner invitations.
- Multi-school access separation.

Digital Skills Wallet

- Learner achievement records.
- Teacher-awarded achievements.
- School-administered achievement verification.
- A centralised record of recognised learner skills.
- A foundation for lifelong learner development.

Digital credentials

- Digital certificate issuing and revocation.
- QR-based certificate verification.
- Downloadable certificate PDFs.
- Downloadable skills transcripts.
- Public certificate verification pages.

Learner portfolios

- Shareable learner portfolio pages.
- Controlled public presentation of learner information.
- Verified achievements and published portfolio content.

Career Discovery

- Career library and career categories.
- Career information and exploration.
- Learner interest selection.
- Subject profiles.
- Career recommendations based on skills, interests and subjects.
- Saved careers.
- Career exploration history.
- Suggestions for skills to develop.

Career matching

The current career-matching model uses a weighted scoring approach:

Matching factor| Weight
Verified skills| 60%
Interests| 25%
Subjects| 15%

The matching system is designed as an educational guidance tool. Recommendations are not guarantees of admission, qualification, employment or career success.

Parent access and notifications

- Learner-controlled parent/family access approval.
- Revocation of approved access.
- In-platform notifications.
- Unread notification indicators.

Analytics and administration

- School analytics.
- National-level analytics interfaces.
- Career content management for authorised platform administrators.
- Achievement and certificate administration.

Architecture principles

FuturePass SA is being developed around the following principles:

Learner-centred development

The learner's achievements, interests, skills and future aspirations form the foundation of the platform.

Verified achievement recognition

The platform distinguishes between learner-provided information, completed work, awarded achievements and verified skills.

Role-based access

Different platform users receive access appropriate to their responsibilities.

School data separation

School-related information and administrative operations are designed around controlled school-level access.

Privacy-conscious development

Learner information is handled with access restrictions, controlled public portfolio information and learner-controlled parent access.

Extensibility

The platform is designed to support additional learning, career and opportunity pathways as development progresses.
